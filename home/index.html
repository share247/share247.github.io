How Share247 Works

Share anything in three quick steps without creating accounts or installing apps.

  1. 01

    Upload, Paste, or Add URL

    Click Upload, drag files into the browser window, press Ctrl+V to paste code/links directly, or open the Share Text modal.

  2. 02

    Set Timer & Optional PIN

    Leave the default 7-day auto-purge or choose a shorter lifetime from 1 minute onward, and optionally protect the entry with a PIN.

  3. 03

    Share Direct Link Anywhere

    Copy the direct /?file=... link or view the shared item straight from the live table on any desktop, tablet, or phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to share files, text, or links on Share247?

No registration or login is required. You can immediately upload files up to 100 MB, paste text or source code, or share a web URL and copy a direct shareable link.

How long do shared files stay visible before automatic deletion?

By default, shared files, text snippets, and links remain visible for 7 days and are automatically deleted once expired. You can also choose custom visibility timers from 1 minute up to 7 days when uploading.

Can I password-protect or PIN-lock a shared file, link, or text?

Yes. Every upload dialog for files, links, and pasted text includes a Sharing Security option where you can set an optional PIN code required before anyone can preview, copy, or download the item.

Which file formats can be previewed directly in the browser?

Share247 previews Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls, .csv), PDF documents, Office files (.docx, .pptx), images, audio and video, and source code or plain text (.txt, .text, .cpp, .c, .py, .js, .json, .md) with line numbers and syntax highlighting.

What is the maximum file size allowed per upload?

You can upload individual files up to 100 MB in size. When overall vault space fills up, older items are automatically cleared to make room for new shares.

How do I share an uploaded file, pasted text, or link with someone else?

Once an item is added to Share247, click Details or Share on the item row to copy its direct link or launch the native share sheet on supported devices.
